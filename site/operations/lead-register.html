Weekly routine and privacy
  1. Check the business inbox each working day; import genuine new notification records.
  2. Review purpose and visitor-provided details. An email address or Turnstile pass is not identity verification. Mark known tests, duplicates or spam.
  3. Assign an owner, next action and due date. Keep company-documentation and support requests separate from sales qualification.
  4. Record the first human response, last meaningful contact and actual outcomes. “Converted” requires a real business result, not just a form submission.
  5. Every week review overdue actions, territory/product gaps and which sources generated qualified conversations. At low volumes, use counts and individual conversations.
  6. Review inactive enquiry records after 12 months. Apply deletion to mailbox copies, register files and exports too, unless an ongoing relationship or applicable obligation requires retention. Keep aggregate monthly summaries for no more than 24 months.
  7. Save the register privately after every session. Back up that folder securely; this tool does not maintain a cloud backup.

No automatic mail replies, CRM synchronisation, form-start tracking or conversion/abandonment measurement is enabled.
